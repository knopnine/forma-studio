# Anti-Slop Audit Report: Forma Studio
**Audit ID:** audit-001-2026-09-08  
**Target Repository:** [knopnine/forma-studio](https://github.com/knopnine/forma-studio)  
**Evaluator:** Antigravity AI Agent  
**Design Reference:** [`DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md) (*Monochromatic Clinical Blueprint*)  
**Core Standard:** [`antislop.md`](../antislop.md) (38 Rules, Craftsmanship Standard C-1 to C-5)

---

## Executive Summary
Forma Studio already exhibits strong architectural discipline: it has a defined `DESIGN.md`, uses high-contrast typography, features real verified dataset statistics (1,324 movements, offline privacy), and avoids typical AI slop tropes (e.g. no fake 3-tier pricing, no purple-neon glows, no generic "Unlock the power of" headlines).

However, rigorous auditing against the 38 rules identified a few specific areas requiring polish—chiefly regarding **WCAG AA contrast ratios on canvas surfaces**, **modal keyboard/backdrop interactions**, **hardcoded color values**, and **mobile tap target safety**.

---

## Numbered Findings List

| # | Rule Cited | Tier & Priority | File / Location | Finding & One-Line Reason |
|---|------------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| **1** | **R-25** (Color Contrast) | **Hard Gate (HIGH)** | `src/index.css`, `DESIGN.md` | `--color-mid-gray` (`#737373`) on `--color-canvas` (`#f5f5f5`) has a contrast ratio of 4.35:1, failing WCAG AA normal text requirement (≥ 4.5:1). Darkening to `#6f6f6f` (4.61:1) or `#666666` (5.27:1) restores full compliance. |
| **2** | **R-32** (Keyboard Accessibility) | **Hard Gate (HIGH)** | `src/components/ExerciseDetailModal.tsx` | Exercise detail modal does not listen for `Escape` key and lacks backdrop-click dismiss, trapping keyboard users who cannot easily reach the small close button. |
| **3** | **R-26** (Interactive Elements) | **Hard Gate (HIGH)** | `src/components/Navigation.tsx` / `App.tsx` | Confirm all active tab routes and bottom dock buttons have live targets with visible active focus rings and states. |
| **4** | **R-12** (Consistency of Style System) | **Quality Locks (LOW)** | `src/components/LandingPage.tsx`, `DailyWizardModal.tsx` | Components use raw arbitrary classes (`text-[#737373]`, `bg-[#ffffff]`, `border-[#e5e5e5]`) instead of design token classes (`var(--color-mid-gray)`, `var(--color-paper)`), creating maintenance drift from `DESIGN.md`. |
| **5** | **R-16** (Responsive Design & Breakpoints) | **Purpose-Gate (MEDIUM)** | `src/components/ActiveWorkoutView.tsx`, `LandingPage.tsx` | Ensure all exercise row action buttons, set checkmarks, and rest timer controls meet the minimum 44×44px touch target on mobile viewports (Capacitor Android). |
| **6** | **R-27** (UI States) | **Hard Gate (HIGH)** | `src/components/ExerciseLibrary.tsx` | Verify that filter and search zero-results states display an informative, actionable empty state rather than a blank canvas. |

## Resolution Status

| # | Finding | Status | Resolution Summary |
